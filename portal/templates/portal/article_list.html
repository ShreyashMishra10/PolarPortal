Polar Glossary

Common terms you will find while reading about the polar regions.

Albedo

How much sunlight a surface reflects. Fresh snow reflects most of it; dark ocean absorbs most of it.

Sea Ice

Frozen ocean water that floats on the sea. It grows in winter and shrinks in summer.

Ice Sheet

A huge mass of land ice covering more than 50,000 km². Only Antarctica and Greenland have them today.

Ice Shelf

A thick floating platform of ice where a glacier or ice sheet flows from land out onto the ocean.

Calving

When a chunk of ice breaks off the edge of a glacier or ice shelf, creating an iceberg.

Permafrost

Ground that stays frozen for at least two years in a row, common across the Arctic.

Ice Core

A long cylinder of ice drilled from an ice sheet. Its layers and air bubbles record past climate.

Polar Night

The period in winter when the Sun does not rise above the horizon for at least a full day.

Midnight Sun

The opposite of polar night: in summer the Sun stays above the horizon for 24 hours or more.

Aurora

Natural light in the polar sky caused by particles from the Sun hitting gases in the upper atmosphere.

Krill

Small shrimp-like animals that are the main food for many whales, seals and penguins in Antarctica.

Arctic Amplification

The fact that the Arctic is warming much faster than the rest of the planet.
