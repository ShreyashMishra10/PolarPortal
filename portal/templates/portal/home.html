Two Poles, Two Worlds

They are both cold and icy, but the Arctic and Antarctic are very different places.

🧭 The Arctic

  • What it is: A frozen ocean surrounded by land
  • Coldest recorded: about −69.6 °C (Greenland)
  • Famous animal: Polar bear
  • People: Around 4 million live in the Arctic region
  • Big concern: Summer sea ice is shrinking fast

🐧 The Antarctic

  • What it is: A frozen continent surrounded by ocean
  • Coldest recorded: −89.2 °C (Vostok Station, 1983)
  • Famous animal: Emperor penguin
  • People: No permanent residents, only researchers
  • Big concern: Ice sheet melt raising sea levels
